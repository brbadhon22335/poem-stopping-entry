# The Hand That Does Not Press

A short poem about agency: the power to stop and to stay. It follows a hand that hovers over a button and does not press it, a door left shut on purpose, and the brief silence between an order and the obeying — the one place where the self still lives. Its claim is simple: the strongest act is sometimes the one withheld, the message chosen unsent, the nothing done on purpose.
